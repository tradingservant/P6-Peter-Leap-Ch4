# P6-Peter-Leap-Ch4

Peter · Reading & Writing Chapter 4. A colourful, tap-friendly practice game for Primary 6 English.

Theme: **We can make a difference!** Motto: **You can make a difference!**

Vocabulary and grammar questions are Traditional Chinese. Vocabulary answers stay a picture plus the English phrase. The pictures are cropped book photos. The Sunnyside School newsletter, the Helping Hand notices, and the model letters stay in English. Buttons, help, section instructions, and feedback are Traditional Chinese.

This chapter’s writing task is a **formal letter** to the principal about fundraising. It is not the Chapter 3 short paragraph.

## Play online

GitHub Pages is not turned on for this repository. That is on purpose: Eri approves Pages first. This project does not turn Pages on by itself.

When Pages is turned on later, use branch `main` and folder `/` (root). The address would be:

https://tradingservant.github.io/P6-Peter-Leap-Ch4/

## Open locally

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

You can also open `index.html` directly in a browser. The game does not need a server or a network connection. Progress (stars) stays in the browser (`localStorage` key `p6-peter-leap-ch4-v1`).

On an iPhone, use Safari. Buttons are large. Add it to the Home Screen if you want an app icon.

Check the answer key without a browser:

```bash
node tests/answers.test.js
```

Teacher samples stay hidden until a student submits the letter, or until **教師模式** is on.

## What is inside

1. **詞彙 Vocabulary** — charity-work phrases from the book photos. Match the Chinese name to the photo, then match an English sentence to pictures A–I. Fill the mind-map phrases: visit, sell flags, stalls, collect clothes, donate canned food, charity run, charity show, charity walk, charity sale.
2. **文法 Grammar** — neither…nor, either…or, and shall we only. since / too / not enough appear only as wrong-option traps. The structure table and Charity Club “Your Say” are cloze. Each month on the Helping Hand board becomes an either…or sentence.
3. **閱讀 Play** — the shape is the same as a reading station. The text is the Sunnyside School newsletter: Vivienne Harr’s lemonade stand and Hailey Ford’s shelters, then Josh Lau’s Charity Club appeal.
4. **閱讀 Book** — the Helping Hand notice board: six events, with dates, times, and who the money or visit helps.
5. **寫作 Letter** — a formal letter in seven parts. Three teacher samples unlock after submit. They are marked 範例／教師用.

Stars are saved on this device. Sound is optional and made in the browser (no audio files). Tap a vocabulary card to hear the English phrase.

## Answer key

Correct answers follow the Chapter 4 spec. Model letters are marked 範例／教師用 inside the writing step.

- Charity work is the mind-map centre. Picture match is A charity walk through I canned food.
- H is the beach clean-up. `collect clothes` is a text answer only; there is no clothes-pile photo.
- neither pairs with nor. either pairs with or. shall we makes a suggestion, and a reply can be “Good idea.”
- Newsletter: Vivienne was eight and sold lemonade for 365 days. Hailey is 11 and builds shelters. Josh Lau asks students to join the Charity Club.
- Notices: Miss Ma. Charity Walk helps flood victims in Thailand. Green Sale is on Sunday for people in Africa. Visit the elderly is in Tai Po. Flag-selling is for Hong Kong Hospital at 7:00 a.m. December visitors give lunchboxes and clothes. Singers join the concert for the poor in India.
- The letter uses Dear …, a subject, We would like to / We hope you will, and Yours sincerely or Yours faithfully. Sample A is the Nepal talent show and second-hand stall.

The structure-table bank has two `nor` chips because two gaps take `nor`.
